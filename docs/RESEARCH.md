Four parallel research passes: Money, Entertainment, Skills, and Zimran itself.
**Reliability note:** most figures come from search-result summaries of the cited pages, not from the primary reports, and many are for all US adults rather than 25–45. Verify any number before it goes on a slide.

## 3. Strongest pains found
### Money
- **Not negotiating pay.** 55% did not negotiate their starting salary; 78% of those who did got a better offer; 57% regret how they handled it (Resume Genius, 2025). https://www.nasdaq.com/articles/over-50-us-workers-dont-negotiate-their-starting-salaries-how-improve-your-offer
- **Side-hustle taxes.** 27% of adults have a side hustle, average $885 / month (Bankrate, 2025). https://www.bankrate.com/press-releases/fewer-americans-have-a-side-hustle-in-2025/ — IRS penalty totals were only found second-hand.
- **BNPL / bills colliding with payday.** 47% of BNPL users paid late in the past year, 63% held several loans at once (LendingTree, 2026). https://www.lendingtree.com/personal/buy-now-pay-later-loan-statistics/ — Cushion, a startup on this pain, shut down in 2025. https://techcrunch.com/2025/01/30/fintech-startup-cushion-shuts-down-after-8-years-and-over-20-million-in-funding
- **Medical bill errors.** 45% of insured working-age adults got a bill they thought should be covered; 38% of disputers had it reduced (Commonwealth Fund, 2024). Episodic need.

### Skills
- 62% of Americans gave up on a new skill in the last 5 years (Preply, 2025). https://preply.com/en/blog/skills-americans-learning-2024
- ~58–60% of new managers get no training (secondary source); 70% avoid hard conversations (secondary).
- Role-play practice is a paid category: Yoodli valued at $300M. https://techcrunch.com/2025/12/05/ex-googlers-yoodli-triples-valuation-to-300m-with-ai-built-to-assist-not-replace-people

### Entertainment
- 12 minutes per session spent choosing what to watch (Gracenote, 2025); 53% say they spend too much time on their phone (Pew, 2026, via secondary); 63% stay up late for "me time" (brand-commissioned poll).
- People pay for AI play: AI Dungeon $9.99–$49.99 / month. https://help.aidungeon.com/faq/membership-plan-overview
- Couples pay ≈ $80 / year for Paired. https://www.paired.com/frequently-asked-questions
